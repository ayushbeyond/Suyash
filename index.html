The artist

For Suyash Goswami, music is more than performing a song. It’s about creating moments that stay with people long after the last note fades.

Through acoustic melodies, familiar tunes and heartfelt performances, he brings a personal touch to every song he sings, from Bollywood favourites to ghazals and Sufi classics.

Whether it’s an intimate acoustic session or a live stage, his approach stays simple: connect with the music, feel every lyric, and let the moment speak for itself.
