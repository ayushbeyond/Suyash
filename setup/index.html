Part 1 · Set up your profile (do this first)

1

Change your profile photo

2 minutes

Download photo
  1. Open Instagram and tap your profile picture (bottom right).
  2. Tap Edit profile → Edit picture or avatar → Choose from library.
  3. Pick the photo you just downloaded and tap Done.

On iPhone: if the photo opens instead of saving, press and hold it, then tap Save to Photos.

2

Update your bio

1 minute

Suyash Goswami Live Singer • Guitarist Bollywood | 90s | 2000s Live music for events & experiences Book an evening: suyash.live
  1. Go to your profile → Edit profile → tap Bio.
  2. Delete the old bio, then press and hold → Paste.
  3. Tap Done (top right).
3

Add your website link

1 minute

https://suyash.live
  1. Edit profile → Links → Add external link.
  2. Paste the link in URL. In Title type: Website & bookings. Tap Done.
  3. Back in Links, press and drag the website so it's first, above the YouTube link.
4

Add WhatsApp to your Contact button

1 minute

  1. Edit profile → Contact options (under "Public business information").
  2. Tap Phone number and enter +91 91655 53087.
  3. Turn on WhatsApp if it asks, then tap Save.

Don't see Contact options? Go to Settings → Account type and tools → switch to a Creator account first. It's free.

6

Share it to your story with a link

2 minutes

  1. Open the reel you just posted → tap the paper plane → Add to story.
  2. Tap the sticker icon (square smiley) → Link.
  3. Paste https://suyash.live, tap Customize sticker text and type Book an evening.
  4. Place the sticker below the video → Your story.

Then for 1 hour: reply to every comment, and ask 5 close friends to send the reel to someone. Sends help reach far more than likes.
