# suyash.live — 15s Enquiry Ad Storyboard (Footage-Only Revision)

**Purpose:** Generate qualified WhatsApp enquiries from people planning celebrations and events in Indore.  
**Format:** Instagram/Facebook Reels, 9:16, 15 seconds.  
**Creative rule:** Use only the footage and stills already supplied in this conversation. No AI-generated crowd, venue, performer, audience reaction, or additional photography.  
**Audio rule:** Suyash's real performance audio is the lead. No external narrator in the master cut.

## Creative direction

The ad should sell the experience of having live music at an event, not sound like a generic artist promo. Start with an event-planning thought, then let the actual performance prove the offer. The edit should feel intimate, confident and premium, with minimal text and a clear WhatsApp action.

## Edit / design specifications

- Canvas: 9:16 vertical. Master delivery: 1080 × 1920.
- Existing MP4s are 512 × 910 px at 30 fps. Upscaling to 1080 × 1920 is delivery formatting only; it will not restore missing detail. Use a high-quality scaler and avoid aggressive sharpening.
- Base grade: restrained warm monochrome / charcoal. Retain one brief authentic purple performance moment as a colour accent if it helps the rhythm. Do not apply a heavy grade that changes skin tone.
- Typography: Urbanist for main copy; DM Mono for small labels; Instrument Serif italic only if one short emotional phrase is genuinely needed.
- Palette: dark charcoal `#120d0a`, warm cream `#efe6db`, muted `#b9a898`, orange `#e8692a`.
- Keep text sparse: one main thought per shot, no long persistent footer, no word-by-word kinetic captions.
- Keep all essential copy within the central safe area. Preview the actual Instagram Reels and Stories placements before publishing; platform UI can cover lower and edge areas.
- Use hard cuts on musical changes. Avoid gratuitous transitions and repeated near-identical crops.

- No genuine audience / crowd footage is present in the supplied usable stage clips. Do not add stock or generated crowd shots and do not claim audience reactions or crowd size.
- The phone clamp / recording phone is visible in the supplied performance angles and can obstruct part of the face. These source files do not contain a clean unobstructed performance angle. A crop may reduce distraction in selected moments, but it cannot remove the phone while preserving the full composition. Do not use generative face reconstruction or fabricate missing facial detail.
- The Ohlio venue sign is visible in several supplied angles. Reframe/crop where practical; if it cannot be removed cleanly without damaging the shot, use a different supplied frame or accept a tighter crop. Do not replace it with invented venue branding.
- The supplied video files are low-resolution previews (512 × 910). Request original camera files before a high-quality paid campaign if they exist. If not, export carefully and do not overstate the quality.
