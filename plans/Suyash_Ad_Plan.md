# suyash.live — Indore Enquiry Ad · Creative Plan (v1, for approval)

**Goal:** get WhatsApp enquiries ("Is my date free?") from people in Indore planning an event.
**Format:** Instagram/Facebook Reels ad, 9:16, 1080×1920, sound on, **15 s main cut** + 6 s Story cut + 3 alternative hooks.
**Objective in Ads Manager:** Engagement → Messaging apps → WhatsApp (the ad's button opens a chat with him).

---

## 1. What the research says (and what changes from the last ad)

| Finding | What we do |
|---|---|
| Meta treats the **first 3 seconds** as the main quality signal ("Hook Rate" = 3-second views ÷ impressions; above 30% is strong). | The first frame is already moving, with a big question on screen and the voiceover starting at 0.0 s. No logo fade-in. |
| **Safe zones:** nothing important in the **top 14%** or the **bottom 35%**, plus 5–10% side margins. | All key text sits between **y 270 and y 1245 px**, with **x margins of 84 px**. ⚠️ The previous ad had its tags and WhatsApp button at y 1180–1340, so the ad's own button and caption covered them. Fixed in this plan. |
| Most people start with the sound off. | Every voiceover line is also written on screen as **burned-in captions**. |
| 15–30 s works for direct response, and the request should come mid-video as well as at the end. | 15 s. A small "WhatsApp your date" pill appears at 7 s, and the full request comes at the end. |
| Click-to-WhatsApp works best in India when the first message is easy to send. | The ad tells people exactly what to send: **date + venue**. The WhatsApp greeting is set up to match. |

---

## 2. Who we're talking to

1. **Wedding / sangeet / engagement families**: the biggest spenders, and they book 1–3 months ahead.
2. **House-party and birthday hosts**: decide fast, from 1 week ahead.
3. **Cafés and restaurants**: want regular weekend nights.
4. **Corporate / HR teams**: office parties and year-end events.

**Targeting:** Indore + 25 km · age 23–50 · interests: weddings, event planning, parties, live music, restaurants · a second ad set for people recently engaged.

---

## 3. Psychology: why each beat is there

| Lever | How it shows up |
|---|---|
| **Mirror their search** | The hook repeats what they're thinking: *"Looking for a live singer in Indore?"* It also qualifies people in the first second, so we pay for fewer wrong clicks. |
| **Problem → contrast** | *"Bluetooth speaker? Your guests deserve live."* It names the cheap option they're comparing against. |
| **Proof (show, don't tell)** | Crowd silhouettes, beams, a real stage, phones up. The live shots do the convincing, and we never claim numbers we can't back up. |
| **Range = less risk** | Ghazal → Bollywood → 90s in a few seconds answers "Will he play what *my* guests like?" |
| **Imagine it** | *"Picture this at your sangeet."* Viewers place the scene in their own event. |
| **Gentle urgency** | *"Wedding-season dates are filling up."* True every Nov–Feb. No fake countdowns. |
| **Make replying easy** | *"Just send your date & venue."* One line to send, and no price question needed. |
| **Brand memory** | The **suyash.live** chip stays on screen the whole time, so it's remembered even without a click. |

## 8. Voiceover

**Voice:** a warm male voice, 25–35, relaxed, like a friend recommending someone. Not a radio announcer. It plays about 30% under the music and is mixed so his singing is never covered.

**English (≈ 14 s):**
> Looking for a live singer in Indore?
> Skip the speaker — your guests deserve live.
> Sangeet, house party, café night… picture this at yours.
> Wedding-season dates are filling up. Just WhatsApp your date and venue.

**Hinglish (recommended for Indore, ≈ 14 s):**
> Indore mein live singer dhoondh rahe ho?
> Speaker chhodo — mehmaanon ko live suna do.
> Sangeet ho, house party ya café night… socho yeh aapki shaam pe.
> Shaadi season ki dates bhar rahi hain. Bas apni date aur venue WhatsApp karo.

**Who records it:** best is Suyash himself (real voice = trust). Otherwise I can generate an AI voiceover, or you record it on a phone in a quiet room and I'll clean it up.

**Button:** Send WhatsApp message.

## 10. Launch and testing

- **Budget:** ₹500/day × 3 hook versions × 5 days ≈ ₹7,500 test → keep the cheapest one and turn the others off.
- **Watch:**
  - Hook rate above 30%
  - Cost per WhatsApp conversation
  - How many chats include a date (that's a real lead)
- **Reply to every chat within 10 minutes.** Speed matters more than the ad.
