## Rules for all three
- **Safe area:** all text between y 270 and 1245 px. Nothing in the bottom 35%.
- **Audio:** his singing always keeps its own audio under its own footage (no home audio over stage pictures). The voice ducks the music 9 dB.
- **Branding:** the suyash.live chip is always on, and every ad uses the same end card.
- **Look:** same palette and fonts as the site. Reel 2 can go warmer (sepia) for the mehfil mood.
- **⚠️ Music rights:** these are covers of Bollywood/ghazal songs. Meta can **mute or reject ads** that use copyrighted songs (covers included). Keep song snippets short (2–4 s each), run the voice-led cuts, and if an ad gets flagged, use the organic version or an original/instrumental piece under it. Boosting an organic post usually has the same issue.
