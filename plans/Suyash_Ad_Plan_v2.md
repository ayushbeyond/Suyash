## 2. Using the Pexels crowd clips honestly

**What they are:**
- **13003413:** a big Indian concert crowd, phones up, faces lit (13.5 s, 1080p).
- **13003417:** arms raised, phone torches, crowd singing (11.7 s).

**The risk:** if we cut *Suyash singing → stock crowd cheering*, it **claims that crowd was at his gig**. That's misleading, and Meta can flag ads for it. It also mismatches the offer: these are **arena-sized crowds**, while he's selling sangeets, house parties and cafés.

**How we use them:**
1. **As the hook, not as a reaction shot.** The first 1.5 s shows torches/hands in the air with *"Planning a celebration in Indore?"*. It's a mood image of "the moment", shown before he appears, so it reads as aspiration rather than proof.
2. **One mid-ad beat framed as the viewer's guests:** *"picture your guests here."* That's honest, because it's about their event.
3. **Crop to hands, phone lights and backs of heads.** Avoid recognisable faces in close-up. Pexels allows commercial use but not suggesting that identifiable people endorse something.
4. **Graded into the same warm black-and-white** as his footage so it doesn't look like stock.
5. **Max ~2.8 s of stock in 15 s.** Over 80% of the ad stays real Suyash.

**Version without stock:** we can also make a cut with no stock footage at all, using only his stage shots. Run it against the stock version and see which gets more enquiries.

## 6. Look and typography (matches suyash.live)

- **Grade:**
  - Warm monochrome: shadows `#120d0a`, highlights cream.
  - Fine grain over everything, so the Pexels footage and the 4K stage footage feel like one shoot.
  - The only colour is the 1.2 s purple hands shot and the orange accents.
- **Fonts:**

  | Use | Font |
  |---|---|
  | Main lines | Urbanist 400–500, lowercase, tight letter-spacing (−0.045em), 104–120 px |
  | Labels | DM Mono, caps, 22 px, letter-spacing 0.15em |
  | One emotional line per shot | Instrument Serif *italic* in orange |

- **Glass:** frosted blur with a bright top-left edge. Used only for the suyash.live chip and the end card, not on every shot.
- **Motion:** text rises 30 px and fades in over 0.45 s, and it's cut on the beat. No fancy transitions.
